# Level Up — Privacy policy

**Level Up** ("the app") is a local-first fitness tracker and progression system (the "Hunter System"). We do not sell your data, we do not run ads, and no training or health data ever leaves your phone unless you explicitly export it.

## What we collect

Nothing on a server. The app stores the following locally on your device only:

- Workout sessions, sets, weights, reps and duration
- Bodyweight measurements and progress photos you choose to add
- Attribute levels, ranks, quests, missions, skill-tree progress and class path
- App settings (theme, units, reminders, notification sounds)

No account is required. No email, name or phone number is ever collected by the app.

## Health Connect

On Android, the app may read and write step counts and activity data through Google Health Connect **only if you enable it in Settings**. Health Connect data stays on your device and is never uploaded anywhere. You can revoke the permission at any time in Android settings or disable Health Connect inside the app.

## Notifications

Local reminders (daily quest board, "streak at risk", weekly missions) are scheduled with `flutter_local_notifications` and fire entirely on-device. No notification content is sent to a server.

## Sharing & export

If you export a CSV or backup, the resulting file is written to your device storage. It is yours to keep, share or delete. The app never transmits it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after changes means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: open an issue at https://github.com/IB1203/LevelUp/issues
